01 · THE PROBLEM

Product discovery is changing.

Search. Marketplaces. Ads. Brand websites. SEO. That was the map. Now imagine asking AI instead.

What's the best sunscreen for oily skin under ₹500?
Here are a few options…
NeutrogenaDot & KeyMinimalist

Where is the brand?

Traditional analytics tell a brand what happens after a customer discovers it. But what about the moment before? What happens when an AI system decides which products enter the recommendation?

WHAT WE ALREADY MEASURE

  • Traffic
  • CTR
  • Conversions
  • Search rankings
  • Campaign performance

THE QUESTION I WANTED TO EXPLORE

  • AI recommendation visibility

If consumers increasingly ask AI what to buy, can we measure whether a product is actually entering those recommendations?

05 · THE KASPARRO CONNECTION

Where I see SignalLens connecting with Kasparro

I built SignalLens as an independent project. While thinking about where the problem could become useful in the real world, I started thinking about Kasparro. This is my hypothesis, not an existing Kasparro product.

What happens before the customer reaches the brand?

If a commerce intelligence company helps brands understand their digital performance, there is an additional question: what happens when the customer asks AI what to buy?

HYPOTHETICAL KASPARRO USE CASE

A skincare brand works with a commerce/growth team that already knows traffic, conversions, search and campaign performance. They want to know: are AI assistants recommending our products? SignalLens could potentially provide brand inclusion, observed position, competitor appearances, buyer-requirement patterns, potential product-information gaps, and the evidence behind each.

The kind of insight I want to create

POTENTIAL VISIBILITY GAP

Requirement: “No white cast” · Observed: brand absent in several prompts containing it · Profile: requirement not explicitly represented.

What SignalLens can say: “This pattern is worth investigating.”

  • “AI hates this product.”
  • “Adding this attribute guarantees recommendations.”
  • “This is definitely why the brand disappeared.”
  • ✓ An observable pattern supported by the tested prompts.
  • ✓ Here is the evidence.
  • ✓ Here is what the team can investigate next.

The same habits Kasparro describes in its engineering doctrine — schemas as contracts, pipelines that fail closed, refusing to emit a number that can't be defended — are the habits I tried to build into this project.

06 · THE FUTURE

My engineering thinking

SIGNALLENS IS MY ATTEMPT TO MAKE THAT QUESTION MEASURABLE.

Why I want to explore this with Kasparro

I didn't build SignalLens because I wanted another portfolio project. I built it because I wanted to explore a problem where AI, commerce, product data and decision-making intersect. Kasparro made me think about how this idea could move from an individual experiment toward a real commerce intelligence use case.

I want to explore the problem. I want to understand the real constraints. I want to test whether this creates measurable value. And I want to build from there.
